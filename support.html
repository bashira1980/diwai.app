🚀 Getting Started

DIWAI stands for Do It With AI. It's an AI-powered DIY assistant that helps you fix, plan, and design home improvement projects. Simply snap a photo or describe what you need, and DIWAI generates step-by-step plans with tools, materials, estimated costs, and safety guidance.
Go to the Projects tab, describe your project or pick one of the suggested ideas (like "Tile bathroom wall" or "Fix leaking tap"), then tap Generate Plan. DIWAI will create a detailed project plan with steps, tools, materials, and estimated costs within seconds.
DIWAI offers three camera modes:

Fix — Diagnose issues by photographing the problem. AI identifies what's wrong and creates a repair plan.
Plan — Create full project plans from photos of spaces or items you want to improve.
Design — AI room redesign that generates three design concepts with before & after mockups. Premium only.

⚡ Features

Take a photo of your room, and DIWAI generates three unique design concepts complete with before & after mockup images. Select your favourite concept and the app creates a full project plan with steps, materials, and costs to bring that design to life.
The AI estimates prices based on major UK retailers such as B&Q, Screwfix, Wickes, and others. Prices are approximate and may vary depending on your location, availability, and market conditions. We recommend checking with your local store for exact pricing.
