Frequently Asked Questions

How does DIWAI work?

Take a photo of a space or item you want to improve. DIWAI uses AI to analyze your image and generate step-by-step DIY project instructions, material lists, and guidance tailored to your skill level.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All communication is encrypted via HTTPS. Your photos are processed by AI to generate project plans and are not shared publicly. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Which sign-in methods are supported?

You can sign in with your Apple ID or Google account.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Contact us at the email below and we will delete your account and all associated data.
