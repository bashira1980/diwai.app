Core Features
Features

Your DIY copilot

Everything you need to tackle any project with confidence.

📷

Snap & Plan

Point your camera at a leaky tap, cracked wall, or tired room. DIWAI analyses the image and generates a full project plan instantly.

🔍

Fix & Diagnose

AI-powered diagnosis with confidence scoring. Identifies what's broken, flags safety concerns, and suggests repair actions with professional referrals when needed.

🎨

AI Design Mode

Visualise renovations before you start. Get three AI-generated design concepts with before & after comparisons and one-tap conversion to project plans.

🔧

Tools & Materials

Detailed lists of exactly what you need — tools, materials, quantities, and estimated costs from real retailers. Share your shopping list or compare prices.

📜

Step-by-Step Guide

Clear numbered steps with time estimates, AI-generated visual guides, pro tips, and per-step checklists. Follow along at your own pace.

✅

Check My Work

Snap a photo of your completed step and let AI verify your work. Get quality assessment and improvement suggestions before moving on.

⚠️

Safety Guidance

Four-level safety system from info to blocker. PPE recommendations, hazard warnings, building regulations, and certified professional referrals.

🎤

Voice Control

Hands-free operation with on-device speech recognition. Navigate steps, repeat instructions, and control your workflow entirely by voice.

📍

Nearby Stores

Find 15 types of suppliers near you — DIY stores, builders merchants, tool hire, timber, tiles, plumbing, and more. One-tap directions.

🎥

Smart Camera

Pro camera with zoom presets (0.5x, 1x, 3x), pinch-to-zoom, front/back switching, and three AI modes from one viewfinder.

🌎

Multi-Region

Tailored for UK, USA, EU, and Australia. Region-specific regulations, local retailer pricing, and measurement unit support.

📂

Project Management

Save unlimited projects, track progress, and pick up where you left off. Rename, organise by room, and manage from your profile.

Do It

Follow each step with detailed instructions, visual guides and safety tips. Track progress and complete like a pro.
