How it works

Three steps.
Zero guesswork.

Point, plan, and do. DIWAI turns any home problem into a clear action plan in seconds.

Step 01

Snap a photo

Point your phone at anything — a leaking tap, a wall you want to paint, a garden you want to redesign. DIWAI identifies the project instantly.

Step 02

Get your plan

AI generates a complete step-by-step guide with tools list, materials with real prices, time estimates, and safety warnings — in seconds.

Step 03

Do the work

Follow the interactive guide step-by-step. Buy materials from nearby stores with live prices. Get AI help when you're stuck.

Features

Everything you need
to DIY smarter.

DIWAI combines AI vision, real pricing, and step-by-step guidance into one tool.

AI Fix Mode

Photograph any problem — a broken hinge, a damp patch, cracked plaster — and get a targeted repair plan instantly.

AI Plan Mode

Describe or photograph your project. Get a complete plan broken into clear, manageable steps with time estimates.

Shopping List with Real Prices

Automatically builds a materials list with live prices from stores near you. No more guessing what things cost.

Nearby Stores

Finds B&Q, Screwfix, Toolstation and more near you. Shows opening hours and distance so you can pick up what you need fast.

Safety Guidance

Every plan includes safety warnings, required PPE, and when to call a professional — so you never take unnecessary risks.

Voice Control

Hands covered in paint? Navigate steps, ask questions, and control the app entirely by voice while you work.

AI Design Mode Premium

Photograph a room and reimagine it completely. AI generates photorealistic redesigns so you can see your vision before you pick up a brush. Compare before and after. Share with friends. Commit with confidence.
