Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026

DIWAI ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use DIWAI:

  • Account information — name and email address, provided when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Photos — images you upload for project analysis. These are sent to our server for AI processing and are not shared with third parties beyond the AI providers used to generate your results.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics such as features used, session duration, and crash reports to help us improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the DIWAI service.
  • To process your DIY project images using AI (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic Claude).
  • To authenticate your account and manage your sessions.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Google Gemini / OpenAI / Anthropic Claude — for AI-powered project analysis. Images and text you submit may be processed by these providers under their respective privacy policies.
  • Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In — for authentication.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS. AI API keys are stored only on our server and are never accessible to the app or end users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

7. Children's Privacy

DIWAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at support@diwai.app.
